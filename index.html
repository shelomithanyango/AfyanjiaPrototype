Why AfyaNjia

Structured coordination with role-restricted access

Every workspace and display terminal requires authorized staff sign-in, while visit tickets move cleanly from reception to completion without duplicate numbers or unprompted audio calls.

01

Registration vs. Calling Separated

Registering a patient generates a persistent ticket (e.g., R025) and queues them for triage—without automatically announcing every arrival on the waiting screen.

02

Intrusion-Protected Terminals

Every department portal and the waiting-room screen require role sign-in so unauthorized visitors cannot alter queues or jump between workspaces.

03

Centralized Audio & Visual Calls

When staff click Call Next Patient, the central waiting screen updates and browser SpeechSynthesis announces the ticket number clearly.

Persistent Ticket Flow

How a patient ticket moves through the dispensary

The ticket number stays the same throughout the visit; only its department, status, and destination update as care progresses.

1. Reception

Register & Issue Ticket

Enters visit reason, generates ticket (e.g., R025), tells patient verbally, and queues for triage.

2. Triage

Call, Assess & Route

Nurse clicks Call Next Patient, records observations, and chooses next destination.

3. Consultation

Call & Decide Care

Clinician calls ticket, records notes, and routes to lab, pharmacy, follow-up, referral, or complete.

4. Laboratory

Call & Process Tests

Lab calls ticket when ready, processes requested tests, and updates completion status.

5. Pharmacy

Call & Dispense

Pharmacy calls ticket, prepares prescribed medicines, and marks visit completed.
